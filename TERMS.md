## Your data is your responsibility

This app keeps your financial records on your device and sends them nowhere. There is no server
copy and no way for anyone else to recover your data for you. If you lose your device, reset it, or
uninstall the app, the data is gone.

**Back up regularly.** Use the backup and CSV export features in Settings and keep the files
somewhere you control. A backup file contains your financial data in readable form, so store it as
carefully as you would a bank statement.

## Accuracy

The app is a manual expense tracker. Balances reflect what has been entered, not what your bank
holds. Use it for your own budgeting, not as a record of account.

The optional message-import feature reads bank SMS on your device and *suggests* transactions. It
can misread a message or miss one entirely, which is why every suggestion is shown to you before
anything is saved. Check what you accept, and treat your bank's own statements as authoritative.
